Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 25, 2026 • PACKAGE: com.nothing.island.ai

1. Zero-Data On-Device Processing

Dot Island runs 100% locally on your Android device. All notification summaries, media playback metadata, active call state indicators, Bluetooth peripheral status, and battery charging telemetry are processed strictly in memory in real time.

Dot Island collects, stores, sells, shares, or transmits zero user data to external servers, third-party analytics providers, or advertising networks.

2. Permissions We Request & Why

  • Display Over Other Apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW): Renders the floating Dynamic Capsule around your camera cutout over active apps.
  • Notification Access (NotificationListenerService): Reads active notifications and media session state locally to render compact and expanded pills. Nothing is logged to disk or sent off-device.
  • Accessibility Service (Optional — AccessibilityService): Used strictly as a display anchor above the status bar and to execute user-configured global actions (such as taking a screenshot). Dot Island never reads screen passwords, never logs keystrokes, and collects zero accessibility data.
  • Bluetooth Connect (Optional): Reads connected audio device name and battery percentage locally for peripheral connection capsules.
  • Answer Phone Calls & Audio Routing (Optional): Allows accepting, declining, or toggling speakerphone on active calls directly from the Call UI capsule.

3. Local Preferences & Retention

Your theme choice, capsule geometry calibration, mascot selection, notification filters, and game-mode settings are saved locally on your device via Android Jetpack DataStore. Uninstalling Dot Island permanently removes all app preferences from your device.

4. Open Source Transparency & Contact

You can inspect the complete source code or report privacy questions via our GitHub repository at github.com/sumitsinghjatav64-creator/nothing-island.
